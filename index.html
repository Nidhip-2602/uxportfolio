About me...

My Goal

I want to design meaningful experiences that make complex interactions feel simple, intuitive, and human. I aim to create solutions that are both useful for people and purposeful for the context they exist in.

My Background

With 2 years of experience as a Data Engineer, my curiosity gradually evolved from solving technical problems to understanding human behavior and the way people interact with systems, leading me toward UX design and human-centered thinking.

My Approach

I believe good design starts with empathy, asking questions, listening closely, and building experiences that feel intuitive and not just screens that look pretty.
