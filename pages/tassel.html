Brand Identity

The Tassel Co.

for Aishwarya Bagane

A brand identity for a home-grown business that creates unique, versatile and affordable saree tassels through creative customisation and intricate handcrafted detailing.

Role

Brand Strategy & Visual Identity Designer

Client

Aishwarya Bagane, Founder & Curator

Scope

Strategy, logo, packaging, stationery & Instagram

Methods

Founder interview, customer research, positioning, moodboarding

Domain

Handcrafted accessories, saree fashion

Overview

The Tassel Co. creates unique, versatile and affordable saree tassel designs through creative customisation and intricate handcrafted detailing.

Challenge

The Tassel Co. needed a distinct brand identity to stand out in the tassel customisation space, communicate its unique handcrafted designs and build a recognisable presence beyond word-of-mouth referrals.

Solution

Developed a cohesive brand identity combining contemporary Indian influences with a bold, vibrant colour palette and playful patterns. The visual language extends across branding, packaging and Instagram, celebrating individuality, creativity and handcrafted detailing.

The business

A home-based, customer-focused business offering customised tassels for individual saree owners.

Primary audience

Individual saree owners

Core differentiator

Customisation

Sales channels

Instagram + referrals

Pricing

Multiple price ranges

Customer research

They had something in mind but were not sure. She gave them the confidence that it would look great.

Early customers discovered the business through word of mouth and recommendations.

They chose her for unique tassels they had never seen before.

Design, quality and price range all mattered in the decision to customise.

Ordering felt easy: they simply DMed on Instagram. They would recommend her, because the designs are trending and very unique.

02 · Strategy

Positioning: three ideas the brand stands on.

01

Customisation

Every saree deserves a personal touch.

We believe every customer has a unique vision. We bring their ideas to life through personalised designs, suggestions and creative combinations.

02

Uniqueness

Designs that stand apart.

We celebrate individuality through distinctive patterns, creative combinations and designs that make every saree feel special.

03

Timelessness

Beyond fleeting trends.

We believe in creating designs that complement the beauty of sarees and remain relevant beyond changing trends and occasions.

03 · Visual direction

Moodboard

A vibrant visual language blending bold colours, playful Indian contemporary patterns and traditional motifs to create a fresh, expressive and culturally rooted identity.

Direction 01 · Heritage

Traditional motifs · Floral patterns · Rich colours · Timeless elegance

Direction 02 · Contemporary

Folk art · Bold colours · Decorative patterns · Contemporary craft

04 · Logo

Explorations: tassels, scrolls and arches.

Pencil sketches of tassel forms, ornamental scrolls, the letter T and Indian arch frames, combined until a single mark emerged.

Final logo

A symmetrical scroll that reads as the letter T when upright, and as a hanging tassel when turned upside down, framed in an Indian arch.

The arch

An Indian window-and-doorway frame that holds the mark like a label, rooting it in the culture of sarees.

The scroll

Ornamental curls inspired by traditional motifs form the arms of a letter T.

The tassel

A fanned tassel crowns the mark, with a single bead at the knot where the two forms meet.
